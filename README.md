### 📈 Quantitative Trading & Market Microstructure

- **[cpp-wasm-limit-order-book](https://github.com/agarwalaashrut/cpp-wasm-limit-order-book)** — A C++17 limit-order-book matching engine compiled to **WebAssembly**, with a React/Vite browser UI for replaying synthetic orders and reconstructed Nasdaq TotalView-ITCH market data. FIFO matching within price levels, multi-symbol routing, portfolio tracking and risk checks, plus live depth, spread, imbalance, walk-the-book slippage, and a WebGL2 liquidity heatmap. *(Group project — Group 11, IE421 HFT, UIUC; mirrored with the team's commit history preserved.)*

- **[Kalshi](https://github.com/agarwalaashrut/Kalshi)** — Research scanners and backtests for pricing inefficiencies in **Kalshi** event-contract markets. Model-free arbitrage on AAA gas-price markets (strike-ladder monotonicity, term structure, YTD-resolved contracts) with an RBOB forecast model, plus a Fed/rates signal stack including ladder arb, rate-vs-futures, and an NLP "Fed tone" model.

- **[blackjack-poker-trainer](https://github.com/agarwalaashrut/blackjack-poker-trainer)** — [**▶ Live demo**](https://agarwalaashrut.github.io/blackjack-poker-trainer/) — Browser-based trainer for blackjack basic strategy, Hi-Lo card counting, and poker equity/EV. Drill decisions against configurable table rules (dealer soft-17, DAS, late surrender), practice a running/true count, and explore starting-hand ranges. React 19 + TypeScript + Vite.

- **[AlpacaTrader](https://github.com/agarwalaashrut/AlpacaTrader)** — Python trading bot on the **Alpaca API**: order execution, paper trading, a backtesting harness, and an ML-augmented strategy.

### 🔬 Systems, Graphics & ML

- **[Sentient-Splat-Slam](https://github.com/agarwalaashrut/Sentient-Splat-Slam)** — Interactive **3D Gaussian-splat (3DGS)** viewer in C++20/OpenGL: real-time rendering of Gaussian point clouds, WASD + mouse-look camera, a JSON scene format, and Python generators for synthetic scenes. This is the rendering front-end for an in-progress monocular **SLAM** pipeline.

- **[brain-to-text-25](https://github.com/agarwalaashrut/brain-to-text-25)** — Preprocessing, models, and experiment tracking for the Kaggle **Brain-to-Text 25** competition.

### 🌐 Full-Stack & Serverless

- **[ai-interview-coach](https://github.com/agarwalaashrut/ai-interview-coach)** — Gemini-powered interview practice platform running entirely on **Cloudflare Workers**. A `ChatSession` **Durable Object** per session owns the conversation history, so the coach remembers prior answers and ramps difficulty across visits. Hono + Chanfana + Zod, with auto-generated OpenAPI 3.1 docs.

- **[EventEase-Public](https://github.com/agarwalaashrut/EventEase-Public)** — [Live](https://event-ease-lovat.vercel.app) — Event coordination platform where groups vote on availability, slots are ranked by aggregated preference scoring, and calendar events are created automatically. I owned the **Google Calendar OAuth 2.0** integration, Flask REST API, MongoDB models and JWT auth, and the email/calendar-sync pipelines. *(Public mirror of my contributions to a private team project.)*

### 🤖 Robotics & Computer Vision

- **[Rover](https://github.com/agarwalaashrut/Rover)** — "Carrier Pigeon Rover": an autonomous Raspberry Pi delivery rover that finds people with **YOLOv8**, roams while avoiding obstacles via ultrasonic sensors, tracks targets on a pan/tilt servo, then scans a QR code to resolve the recipient and emails them the captured photo.
