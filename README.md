### 🚀 Featured Projects

**Quantitative trading**

- **[cpp-wasm-limit-order-book](https://github.com/agarwalaashrut/cpp-wasm-limit-order-book)** — A C++17 limit-order-book matching engine compiled to **WebAssembly**, with a React/Vite browser UI for replaying synthetic orders and real Nasdaq TotalView-ITCH market data. Live depth, spread, imbalance, walk-the-book slippage, and a WebGL2 liquidity heatmap. *(Originally an IE421 HFT group project — Group 11, UIUC.)*
- **[Kalshi](https://github.com/agarwalaashrut/Kalshi)** — Research scanners and backtests for pricing inefficiencies in **Kalshi** event-contract markets: model-free arbitrage on gas-price markets plus a Fed/rates signal stack (ladder arb, rate arb, term structure, and an NLP "Fed tone" model).
- **[AlpacaTrader](https://github.com/agarwalaashrut/AlpacaTrader)** — A Python algorithmic trading bot on the **Alpaca API** with order execution, paper trading, backtesting, and an ML-augmented strategy.
